4. DATA QUALITY NOTES
================================================================================
- Content was scraped as raw prose; no structured field extraction yet.
  Steps, worked_example, when_to_use etc. are all null — pipeline pass pending.
- Encoding artefacts present in a small number of condition names from
  Mental Health Foundation (em-dashes mangled). Will be cleaned at pipeline.
- Duplicate condition entries exist across sources (e.g. "Depression" from
  NHS, NIMH, WHO, MHF, Beyond Blue). This is intentional — multi-source
  coverage improves retrieval diversity. Deduplication happens at chunk level
  via embedding similarity, not at record level.
- The "other" section label (102 records) needs reclassification.
  Will be resolved by a heading keyword pass during ingestion.
- icd11_code is null for some conditions (Self-Harm, Stress, Loneliness,
  Sleep Problems, Paranoia) — these don't have discrete ICD-11 disorder codes.
